Large orders and business customers

There is no minimum order value. For orders of more than 10 pieces of the same item, or for company clothing, write to business@nordvik-outdoor.example for a quote; business customers receive an invoice with VAT ID and can pay on 14-day terms after credit approval.

Standard orders are limited to 10 pieces per item to keep sizes available for all customers.
