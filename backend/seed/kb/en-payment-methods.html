Payment methods

We accept the following payment methods in all EU countries: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay.

Depending on your country you can also pay with:

  • iDEAL in the Netherlands.
  • Bancontact in Belgium.
  • SEPA direct debit in Germany, Austria and the Netherlands.
  • Klarna pay later in Germany, Austria, the Netherlands and Belgium.

We do not accept cash on delivery, bank transfer in advance, cheques or cryptocurrencies.
