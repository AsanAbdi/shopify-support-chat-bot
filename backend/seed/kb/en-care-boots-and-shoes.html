Caring for boots and shoes

  • Remove laces and insoles, brush off dirt and rinse with lukewarm water. Never use a washing machine.
  • Let shoes dry at room temperature. Stuff them with newspaper. Never put them on a radiator or in direct sun, as heat can crack leather and dissolve glue.
  • Treat leather boots with leather wax or conditioner every 2 to 3 months. Do not wax nubuck; use a nubuck spray.
  • Waterproof membranes (GORE-TEX) work best when the outside material is clean.
