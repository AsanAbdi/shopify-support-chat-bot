Change or cancel an order

You can change the delivery address, swap sizes or cancel an order as long as it has not been dispatched. We pack orders within a few hours on working days, so contact support as soon as possible and mention your order number.

After dispatch

Once an order has been dispatched it can no longer be changed or cancelled. You can refuse the delivery, or accept it and return the items within 30 days for a refund (see Returns).

What we cannot do

  • We cannot add items to an existing order. Place a new order instead; shipping is calculated per order.
  • The chat assistant can only look up orders. Changes and cancellations are handled by our support team.
