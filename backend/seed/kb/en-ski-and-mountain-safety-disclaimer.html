Technical gear and safety

Our clothing and equipment are designed for hiking, trail running and everyday use. They are not certified personal protective equipment, for example for climbing, avalanche rescue or work at height.

Trekking poles, headlamps and packs are tested for normal use. Check the weight limits and instructions on the product page and the label, and replace equipment after heavy impact.
