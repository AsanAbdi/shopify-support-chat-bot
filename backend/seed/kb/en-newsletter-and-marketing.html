Newsletter and marketing emails

The newsletter is sent about once a week. We use double opt-in: you must confirm your address with a link before you receive emails or a welcome discount.

You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in every email or in your account. Order and shipping emails are service messages and cannot be disabled while an order is open.
