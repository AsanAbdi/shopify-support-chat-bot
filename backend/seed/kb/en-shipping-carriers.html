Our shipping carriers

We ship with three carriers, depending on the destination:

  • PostNL for the Netherlands and Belgium.
  • DHL for Germany, Austria and Luxembourg.
  • DPD for France and all other EU countries.

You cannot choose the carrier. The tracking link in your shipping confirmation always points to the right carrier. Parcels are sent in plain cartons made from recycled material.
