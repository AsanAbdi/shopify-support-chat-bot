Do you have a store?

Nordvik Outdoor is an online shop. We have no physical stores, outlets or showrooms, and the warehouse in Venlo is not open to visitors.

To try before you buy, order your usual size and a second size, and return the one that does not fit within 30 days. Returns from the Netherlands, Belgium and Germany are free.
