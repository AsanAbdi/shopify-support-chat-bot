Return policy: 30 days

You can return items within 30 days after delivery, no reason required. This is longer than the 14-day legal right of withdrawal in the EU, which of course also applies to you.

Condition of returned items

  • Unworn, unwashed and with all tags attached.
  • In the original packaging if possible.
  • Footwear may be tried on indoors only. Shoes with signs of outdoor use cannot be accepted.

Items that cannot be returned

Socks, underwear and opened neck tubes for hygiene reasons, gift cards, and items marked Final sale. Defective items can always be returned under the warranty.
