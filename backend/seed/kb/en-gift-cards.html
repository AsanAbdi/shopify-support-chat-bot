Gift cards

Digital gift cards are available in amounts from EUR 25 to EUR 250. They are sent by email immediately or on a date of your choice.

  • Valid for 3 years from the purchase date, for all products and shipping costs.
  • Cannot be exchanged for cash and cannot be returned once delivered.
  • You can combine a gift card with other payment methods. Check the balance at any time with the code in the Gift card page.

If you did not receive the gift card email, check your spam folder first and then contact support with the order number.
