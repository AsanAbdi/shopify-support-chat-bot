Right of withdrawal (14 days) and withdrawal form

Under EU consumer law you can withdraw from a distance purchase within 14 days after you receive the goods, without giving a reason. Our 30-day return policy goes beyond this.

  • Tell us clearly that you withdraw, for example with the returns portal, by email, or using the model withdrawal form in our terms.
  • Return the goods within 14 days after your declaration.
  • We refund all payments, including standard shipping costs, within 14 days after we receive your declaration. We may wait until we have received the goods.

You bear the direct costs of returning the goods only where this is not free under our policy. For items you used beyond inspection, we may deduct a compensation for the loss in value.
