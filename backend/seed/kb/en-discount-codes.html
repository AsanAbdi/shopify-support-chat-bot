Discount codes

New customers get 10% off their first order when they sign up for our newsletter. The code arrives by email after you confirm your address and is valid for 30 days.

  • Only one discount code can be used per order. Codes cannot be combined with each other.
  • Codes do not apply to sale items marked Final sale, to gift cards or to shipping costs.
  • If a code does not work, check the expiry date and the minimum order value in the email, and make sure the cart contains no excluded items.

Codes cannot be added after the order has been placed. We do not match competitors' prices and do not refund price differences after purchase. We currently have no student, military or loyalty discount.
