Where is my order? Order status and tracking

As soon as your order is dispatched you receive a shipping confirmation email with the carrier and a tracking link.

Order statuses

  • Unfulfilled: we have your order and are preparing it.
  • Partially fulfilled: part of your order has shipped; the rest follows in a separate parcel.
  • Fulfilled: all parcels have been handed to the carrier.
  • On hold: we are waiting for a payment confirmation.

Tracking

Tracking information can take up to 24 hours to appear after dispatch. Logged-in customers see all orders under My account. In this chat you can give me your order number and the email address used for the order and I will look up the current status.
