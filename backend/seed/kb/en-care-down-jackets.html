Washing a down jacket

Wash down jackets at 30 degrees on a gentle cycle with a special down detergent. Do not use fabric softener or normal detergent, which strips the natural oils from the down.

  • Use a front-loading machine with plenty of space and an extra rinse.
  • Dry in a tumble dryer on low heat with two or three clean tennis balls or dryer balls. Stop every 30 minutes and shake the jacket.
  • The jacket must be completely dry before storage; this can take 2 to 3 hours. Damp down smells and loses loft.
  • Store uncompressed in a breathable bag, not in the stuff sack.
