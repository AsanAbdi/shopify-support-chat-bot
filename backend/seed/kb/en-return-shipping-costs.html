Return shipping costs

  • Returns from the Netherlands, Belgium and Germany are free of charge.
  • Returns from all other EU countries cost EUR 6.90, which we deduct from your refund.
  • Exchanges are always free of charge in all countries.

If you received a wrong or defective item, the return is free in every country. In that case choose Defective or wrong item as the reason in the returns portal.
