Warranty and legal guarantee

All products carry the legal guarantee of 2 years from delivery that applies to consumers in the EU. If an item is defective, we repair it, replace it or refund it, in this order, free of charge.

  • Defects that appear within the first year are presumed to have existed at delivery.
  • Normal wear (worn-out soles, faded colours after many washes), damage from misuse, and damage from incorrect care are not covered.
  • Own-brand Nordvik jackets also carry a 5-year guarantee on seams and zips. It extends and never limits your legal rights.

We reply to warranty claims within 3 working days.
