Materials and sustainability

  • Recycled polyester: at least 70% of the polyester in our own-brand jackets and fleeces comes from recycled bottles and fishing nets.
  • PFC-free water repellency: all Nordvik outer fabrics use PFC-free DWR since 2023.
  • Down: all down is certified to the Responsible Down Standard (RDS). We do not use down from live-plucked birds.
  • Merino wool: sourced from mulesing-free farms.

Our parcels are packed in recycled cartons and our returns are consolidated to reduce transport emissions. Details are published in our annual sustainability report.
