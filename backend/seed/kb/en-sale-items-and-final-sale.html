Returning sale items

Sale items follow the same 30-day return policy as full-price items. Your refund is the amount you paid, including any discount.

Items labelled Final sale (usually the last pieces of a season) are excluded from returns and exchanges. The label is shown on the product page and in the cart. Final sale items always keep the 2-year legal warranty.

Discount codes do not apply to Final sale items. If you used a code on an order and return part of it, the discount is divided proportionally across the items.
