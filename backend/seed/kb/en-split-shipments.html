Why did my order arrive in several parcels?

Items in one order can be sent in separate parcels, for example when an item is stored in a different location or a size is delivered to the warehouse later. Each parcel has its own tracking number and its own carrier label.

You only pay shipping once per order. Your order status shows Partially fulfilled until all parcels are on their way. If a parcel is missing after the other parcels arrived, wait 2 working days and then contact support.
