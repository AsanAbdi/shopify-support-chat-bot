I lost my return label

You can print or download the label again at any time in the returns portal with your order number and email address. The QR code in your email works at PostNL and DHL points too.

If you already sent the parcel without our label, send us the carrier's proof of postage and the tracking number so that we can find your return. Without a registered return we cannot trace the parcel.
