Contact and opening hours

Our customer support team is available from Monday to Friday between 9:00 and 17:00 CET, except Dutch public holidays. We answer emails within 24 hours on working days.

  • Email: support@nordvik-outdoor.example
  • Chat assistant: available 24 hours a day and can hand over to a colleague.
  • We do not offer a telephone hotline, WhatsApp or social media support.
  • We have no physical stores; the address is for letters only: Nordvik Outdoor B.V., Keizersgracht 100, 1015 AA Amsterdam, the Netherlands.
