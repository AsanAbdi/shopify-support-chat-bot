Delivery times

Orders placed on working days (Monday to Friday) before 14:00 CET are dispatched the same day from our warehouse in Venlo, the Netherlands. Later orders and weekend orders are dispatched on the next working day.

Delivery time after dispatch

  • Netherlands, Belgium, Luxembourg: 1 to 2 working days.
  • Germany, Austria, France: 2 to 4 working days.
  • All other EU countries (for example Italy, Spain, Poland, Denmark, Sweden): 4 to 7 working days.

Delivery times are estimates given by the carrier. Public holidays and peak periods such as Black Friday and December can add a day or two. We do not deliver on Sundays.
