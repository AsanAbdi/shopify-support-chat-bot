Card payments and 3-D Secure

European regulation (PSD2) requires strong customer authentication for most card payments. After entering your card details you are redirected to your bank to confirm with your banking app, a fingerprint, or an SMS code.

We never see or store your full card number: payments are processed by our certified payment provider. Never send your card details in the chat or by email.
