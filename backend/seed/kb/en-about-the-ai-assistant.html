About our AI assistant

The chat on our website is an AI assistant. It answers questions from this Help Center, looks up your order status when you give your order number and email address, and searches our products.

  • It cannot change or cancel orders, create returns or refund payments. A colleague does that.
  • If it does not know the answer, it says so and offers to hand over to a colleague; you then get an email reply within 24 hours on working days.
  • Do not share card numbers or passwords in the chat.
  • Chats are stored for 90 days to resolve your request and improve our service, then deleted automatically.

AI answers can contain mistakes: for legal or financial decisions please confirm with our support team.
