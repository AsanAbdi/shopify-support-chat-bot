Invoices and VAT

Your invoice is attached to the shipping confirmation email and is available in your account under Orders. Prices include VAT.

  • VAT is charged at the rate of the country the goods are delivered to: Germany 19%, Netherlands 21%, Belgium 21%, Austria 20%, France 20%.
  • Business customers enter the company name and VAT ID at checkout. A VAT ID cannot be added after the invoice has been issued.
  • If you need a corrected invoice, contact support within 30 days of the order.
