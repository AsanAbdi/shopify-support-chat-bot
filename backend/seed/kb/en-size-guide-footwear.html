Footwear size guide

All our shoes and boots are sized in EU sizes. The size chart in the CSV document shows EU, UK and US sizes and the matching foot length in centimetres.

How to measure

  1. Stand on a sheet of paper and mark your heel and your longest toe.
  2. Measure the distance in centimetres at the end of the day, when feet are slightly larger.
  3. Add 0.5 to 1.0 cm for comfort and compare with the chart.

Fit by model

  • Trail Runner Pro and Road Racer run half a size small: order half a size up if you are between sizes.
  • Hiking boots and winter boots fit true to size and leave room for thick socks.
  • Approach shoes fit snug by design.
  • Sneakers and sandals fit true to size.
