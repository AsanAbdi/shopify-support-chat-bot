Caring for waterproof jackets

  • Wash at 30 degrees with a mild liquid detergent, without fabric softener. Run an extra rinse.
  • Close all zips and Velcro before washing.
  • Tumble dry on low heat for 20 minutes: warmth reactivates the water-repellent finish (DWR).
  • Do not dry clean and do not iron.

Dirt and body oils reduce breathability, so wash your jacket when it looks dirty or after about 10 days of use. Regular washing is good for the membrane.
