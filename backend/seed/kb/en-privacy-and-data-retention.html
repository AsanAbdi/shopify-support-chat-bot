Privacy and data retention

Nordvik Outdoor B.V. processes your data to fulfil orders, provide support and, with your consent, for marketing. Our servers are located in the European Union.

  • Order data is kept for as long as the law requires, up to 10 years for invoices.
  • Chat conversations are deleted after 90 days.
  • You can ask for access, correction, export or deletion at privacy@nordvik-outdoor.example. We respond within 30 days.
  • Support conversations may be processed by our AI provider on our behalf under a data processing agreement.
