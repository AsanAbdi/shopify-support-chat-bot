Delivery to islands and remote areas

We deliver to all EU addresses, including the Wadden Islands in the Netherlands, the German North Sea islands, Corsica and the Balearic Islands. There is no surcharge.

Island and remote deliveries can take 1 to 3 additional working days because of ferry schedules. The estimated delivery time is shown in your shipping confirmation.

We do not deliver to the Canary Islands, Madeira, the Azores or other EU regions outside the customs territory, because additional customs documents would be required.
