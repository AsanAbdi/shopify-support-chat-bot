Wrong, missing or damaged items

If your parcel is incomplete, contains the wrong item or arrived damaged, please contact support within 14 days of delivery. Send your order number, a photo of the parcel label, the content and any damage.

  • Missing or wrong item: we send the correct item free of charge or refund it, as you prefer.
  • Damaged in transit: report visible damage to the carrier when you receive the parcel and tell us within 48 hours. We send a replacement at no cost.
  • Items that arrive in several parcels: check the shipping confirmation, it lists every parcel with its own tracking number.
