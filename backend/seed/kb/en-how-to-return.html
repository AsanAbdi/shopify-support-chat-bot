How to return an item

  1. Open the returns portal on our website and enter your order number and the email address of the order.
  2. Select the items you want to return and choose the reason.
  3. Choose Refund or Exchange.
  4. Print the prepaid return label, or use the QR code at a PostNL or DHL point.
  5. Pack the items securely and hand over the parcel to the carrier within 7 days.

Keep the carrier's proof of postage until your refund has been paid. We cannot accept returns without a registered return in the portal.
