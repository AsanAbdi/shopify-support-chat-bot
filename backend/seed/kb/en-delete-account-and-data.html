Delete your account and personal data

You can delete your account under Account > Settings > Delete account, or ask us by email at privacy@nordvik-outdoor.example. We delete your profile and saved addresses within 30 days.

  • Invoices and order records must be kept for tax reasons for up to 10 years; they are removed from marketing and from your account.
  • Chat conversations with our assistant are deleted automatically after 90 days. On request we delete the chats linked to your email address earlier.
  • You also have the right to access, correct and export your data. Write to the same address.
