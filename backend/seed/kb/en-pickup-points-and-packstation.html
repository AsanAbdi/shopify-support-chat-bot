Delivery to pickup points and Packstation

Instead of your home address you can have your order delivered to a pickup point. This is available in the Netherlands and Belgium (PostNL service points and parcel lockers) and in Germany (DHL Paketshop and Packstation).

  1. Choose Pickup point at checkout.
  2. Search by postcode and select a location.
  3. For a DHL Packstation enter your DHL customer number (Postnummer).

You receive a notification when your parcel can be collected. Parcels are held for 7 days. Bring a photo ID. Pickup points are not available in other countries.
