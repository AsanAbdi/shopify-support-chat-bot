I did not receive an order confirmation

The confirmation email is sent within a few minutes after payment and contains your order number.

What to check

  • Look in your spam or promotions folder; the sender is orders@nordvik-outdoor.example.
  • Check that you typed your email address correctly. You can see it in your account under Orders if you were logged in.
  • If the payment was declined or cancelled, no order was created and no email is sent. Your bank may show a pending authorisation that disappears by itself within 3 to 7 working days.

If you paid but have no confirmation after 1 hour, contact support with the time of the payment and the last four digits of your payment method.
