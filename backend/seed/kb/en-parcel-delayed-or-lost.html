My parcel is delayed or lost

First check the tracking link in your shipping confirmation. A status like Delayed or Attempted delivery usually resolves within a day.

If the parcel has not arrived

  • If your parcel has not arrived 5 working days after the latest estimated delivery date, contact support with your order number.
  • We then start a trace with the carrier. This takes up to 7 working days.
  • If the parcel cannot be found we send a replacement free of charge or refund the order, whichever you prefer.

Please also check with neighbours and in your parcel box: carriers sometimes leave a parcel without a notification.
