Change the delivery address

The delivery address can be changed free of charge until the order is dispatched. Write to support@nordvik-outdoor.example with your order number and the new address, or ask the chat assistant to hand over to a colleague.

Once the parcel has been handed to the carrier, we cannot change the address. In that case use the carrier's tracking page: PostNL, DHL and DPD offer delivery to a neighbour, a pickup point or a new date. Redirecting to a different address after dispatch is not possible.

Orders to a different country cannot be redirected, because shipping costs and VAT depend on the destination country.
