When water soaks into the fabric: re-proofing

If water no longer beads off the outer fabric, the water-repellent finish (DWR) has worn off. This is normal and does not mean that the membrane has failed. A jacket is still waterproof when the outside gets wet; it just feels cold and clammy.

  1. Wash the jacket and tumble dry on low heat first. Often this is enough.
  2. If water still soaks in, apply a PFC-free wash-in or spray-on re-proofing agent after washing, as instructed on the pack.
  3. Repeat roughly every 20 washes or once per season for heavy use.
