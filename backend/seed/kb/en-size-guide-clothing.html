Clothing size guide

Our clothing chart lists chest, waist and hip measurements in centimetres for sizes XS to 3XL. Measure over thin underwear and compare with the table.

  • Jackets have a regular fit with room for a midlayer. Choose one size up if you want to wear a thick sweater or a down vest underneath.
  • Merino base layers fit snug, as they should against the skin. Order your normal size.
  • Fleeces and hoodies have a relaxed fit.
  • Hiking pants are cut regular with a stretch waist; the inseam is 81 cm in size M.

Between two sizes? Take the larger one for outer layers and the smaller one for base layers.
