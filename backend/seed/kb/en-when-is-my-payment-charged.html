When is my payment charged?

  • Cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, iDEAL and Bancontact: charged immediately when you place the order.
  • Klarna pay later: nothing is charged at checkout. You receive an invoice from Klarna after dispatch and have 30 days to pay.
  • SEPA direct debit: debited within 7 days after dispatch.

If an item is out of stock after you paid, we only charge for what we ship and refund the rest within 5 working days.
