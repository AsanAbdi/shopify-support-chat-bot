Caring for merino wool and fleece

Merino wool: wash at 30 degrees on the wool cycle with wool detergent, inside out. Do not tumble dry; lay flat to dry. Merino resists odours, so air it out and wash less often.

Fleece: wash at 30 degrees with a low spin speed and no fabric softener, which clogs the fibres. Tumble dry on low heat or air dry. Do not iron.

Merino socks: wash at 30 degrees, inside out, and do not tumble dry.
