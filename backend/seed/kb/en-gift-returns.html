Returning a gift

If you received a gift from Nordvik Outdoor, you can return or exchange it within 30 days after delivery. Select Gift receipt in the returns portal, or enter the order number printed on the packing slip.

  • The refund is issued as a gift card to the person returning the item, not to the buyer.
  • The buyer's payment details and the price are never shown to you.
  • When the giver chose the gift-receipt option at checkout, the packing slip does not show prices.
