My payment failed

If your payment failed, no order has been created and nothing is charged. Your bank may show a pending reservation that is released automatically within 3 to 7 working days.

  • Check the card number, expiry date and security code.
  • Card payments require authentication (3-D Secure) in your banking app or by SMS. If the confirmation times out, try again.
  • Check your limit or try another payment method.
  • Disable ad blockers that can interrupt the redirect to your bank or PayPal.

If you were charged twice, contact support with the order number and the date. We check the payment and refund duplicates.
