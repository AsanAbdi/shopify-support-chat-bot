Which countries do you ship to?

We ship to all 27 EU member states. We currently do not ship to the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein or any country outside the EU, and we do not offer shipping to parcel forwarders.

The reason is customs: outside the EU, import duties, import VAT and additional paperwork would apply, which we cannot handle yet. We are evaluating a UK and Swiss service.

You can order with a delivery address in an EU country as a gift for someone abroad.
