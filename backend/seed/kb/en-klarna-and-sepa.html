Klarna pay later and SEPA direct debit

Klarna pay later

Available in Germany, Austria, the Netherlands and Belgium for orders up to EUR 750. You pay 30 days after dispatch by bank transfer or in the Klarna app. Klarna runs a credit check; the decision is made by Klarna.

SEPA direct debit

Available in Germany, Austria and the Netherlands. You authorise us to debit your IBAN. The amount is debited within 7 days after dispatch. If a debit is returned, we ask you to pay by another method.
