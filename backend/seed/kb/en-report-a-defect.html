How to report a defect

  1. Write to support@nordvik-outdoor.example or use the chat to hand over to a colleague.
  2. Include your order number, a description of the problem and 2 to 3 photos: the whole item, the defect, and the label inside.
  3. We answer within 3 working days and send a prepaid label if the item needs to come back.

If we confirm a defect, we repair or replace the item. If neither is possible within a reasonable time, you receive a full or partial refund. Return shipping for warranty cases is always free of charge.
