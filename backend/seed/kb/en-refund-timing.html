When will I get my refund?

When your parcel arrives at our warehouse we inspect it within 2 working days. Your refund is then paid out within 5 working days.

Refund method

  • Refunds always go back to the original payment method.
  • PayPal: usually within a few hours after we release it.
  • Cards, iDEAL, Bancontact, SEPA debit: your bank needs 3 to 5 additional working days to show the money.
  • Klarna: your invoice is adjusted automatically.
  • Orders paid fully or partly with a gift card: the gift card amount is refunded to a new gift card.

The original standard shipping costs are refunded when the complete order is returned.
