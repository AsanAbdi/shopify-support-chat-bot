Cleaning backpacks

Do not put backpacks into a washing machine: foam and coatings can be damaged. Empty all pockets, brush out dirt and clean with lukewarm water, a soft sponge and mild soap.

Dry open and hanging, away from direct sunlight and heat. Keep zips clean with a soft brush and rub the teeth with a bit of candle wax or zip lubricant if they stick.
