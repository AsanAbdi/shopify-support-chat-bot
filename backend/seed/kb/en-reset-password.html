Reset your password

Click Account, then Forgot password, and enter the email address of your account. We send a reset link that is valid for 1 hour.

  • No email? Check your spam folder and make sure you used the same address as when you registered.
  • The link works once. Request a new one if it has expired.
  • For your safety our support team can never see or tell you your password.
