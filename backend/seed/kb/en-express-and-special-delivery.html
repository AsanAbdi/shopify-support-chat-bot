Express delivery, Saturday and same-day delivery

We do not offer express, same-day, Saturday or evening delivery. Standard shipping is the only service, with the delivery times shown in the article Delivery times.

If you need an item urgently, order before 14:00 CET on a working day and choose a pickup point in the Netherlands, Belgium or Germany: these parcels are often available a day earlier.

We have no physical stores or click and collect service.
