How to place an order

Ordering at Nordvik Outdoor takes a few minutes and works with or without an account.

Steps

  1. Choose a product, select colour and size and click Add to cart.
  2. Open the cart, check the items and enter a discount code if you have one.
  3. Enter your delivery address. We ship to EU countries only.
  4. Choose a shipping method and a payment method.
  5. Check the summary and click Place order. Your payment method may ask you to confirm the payment.

After ordering

You receive an order confirmation by email within a few minutes. Items in your cart are not reserved until you have paid. Popular sizes can sell out while you check out.
