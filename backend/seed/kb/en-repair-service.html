Repair service

We repair zips, seams, buckles and torn fabric on Nordvik products, even after the warranty has ended.

  • Flat fee of EUR 15 for seams and small tears, EUR 25 for replacing a zip, EUR 35 for a main zip on jackets over 400 g.
  • Repairs take about 2 to 3 weeks including shipping.
  • Send the clean item with the repair form from our website to our repair workshop in Venlo.

If a repair is not possible, we tell you before charging you.
