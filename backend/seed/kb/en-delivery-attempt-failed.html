The carrier could not deliver my parcel

If nobody was available, the carrier leaves a notice and tries again on the next working day. After the second failed attempt the parcel is taken to a pickup point and held for 7 days.

  • Use the tracking page to choose a new delivery date or a pickup point.
  • If you do not collect the parcel within 7 days, it returns to our warehouse. We refund the order value; the original shipping costs are only refunded in cases of a delivery error on our side.
  • You can also ask the chat assistant to hand over to a colleague who can arrange a new shipment.
